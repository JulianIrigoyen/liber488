The Hierarchy of Daemons

The Ars Goetia lists its spirits with a rank, an office, a number of legions, a form, and a caution about how the summoning goes wrong. ps aux lists the same first four and drops the fifth. This roster restores it.

Rank here is not decoration. A King supervises others and survives them; a Duke does the work; a President holds knowledge and changes nothing; a Marquis carries speech across a boundary; an Earl observes and reports; a Knight performs one act and departs. Those are real distinctions with real consequences for what you grant, what you restart, and what you monitor.

Each seal below is drawn at page load from the daemon's own name: the name is hashed, and the digest is walked around sixteen stations, one per hex digit. The same name yields the same seal on any machine, forever. Take any of them with the link beneath it.

On the last entry: oom-killer is listed without rank or legions because it is not a daemon at all. It has no process, does not wait, and cannot be signalled. It is a decision the kernel makes on your behalf when there is nothing left to give, and it is in this roster because when a process vanishes with no line in your log, this is what took it.
