The Oracle of Six Lines

A hexagram is six binary lines. So is a health check. Six services, each either answering or not, give exactly sixty-four states - and a book of sixty-four judgments is a form the tradition already worked out and indexed.

Read from the bottom upward, as the oracles are read. A whole line answers; a broken line does not. You may cast the lines and let them fall, which is the traditional use, or set them to what your own system is actually doing, which turns the book into a lookup table. It was always a lookup table.

Set the six lines

Which services answer?

Switch each line to match your own system, or cast and let them fall where they will.

Why these six

They are the six stages every message passes through, in order, and each one fails in a way the others cannot cover for. The ordering is the diagnostic: a fault at line three means everything above it is idle and healthy, and restarting any of it will teach you nothing.
